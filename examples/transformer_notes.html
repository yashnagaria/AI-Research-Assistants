Reading notes: "Attention Is All You Need" (Vaswani et al., 2017)

Summary

The paper introduced the Transformer, a sequence-to-sequence model built entirely on attention mechanisms, with no recurrence and no convolutions. It was written by researchers at Google Brain and Google Research and presented at NeurIPS 2017. Removing recurrence lets the model process all positions of a sequence in parallel, which made training much faster than the recurrent models that were state of the art at the time.

Architecture of the base model

  • An encoder stack of 6 identical layers and a decoder stack of 6 identical layers.
  • Each encoder layer has two sub-layers: multi-head self-attention and a position-wise feed-forward network.
  • Each decoder layer adds a third sub-layer that performs multi-head attention over the encoder output. Decoder self-attention is masked so a position cannot attend to later positions.
  • Every sub-layer is wrapped in a residual connection followed by layer normalization.
  • Model dimension d_model = 512, feed-forward inner dimension d_ff = 2048, and 8 attention heads.
  • Because there is no recurrence, sinusoidal positional encodings are added to the input embeddings to give the model information about token order.

Scaled dot-product attention

Attention is computed as softmax(QK^T / sqrt(d_k)) V, where Q, K and V are the query, key and value matrices. The scaling by the square root of the key dimension d_k keeps the dot products from growing so large that the softmax saturates. Multi-head attention runs several attention functions in parallel on different learned projections and concatenates the results, letting the model attend to information from different representation subspaces.

Training setup

The models were trained on one machine with 8 NVIDIA P100 GPUs. The base model trained for 100,000 steps, about 12 hours. The big model trained for 300,000 steps, about 3.5 days. Training used the Adam optimizer with a learning-rate schedule that warms up linearly for 4,000 steps and then decays, plus dropout and label smoothing for regularization.

Results

Both scores were new state-of-the-art results for single models, reached at a fraction of the training cost of earlier approaches. The authors also showed the Transformer generalizes to English constituency parsing.

Why it matters

The Transformer became the foundation of later language models such as BERT and the GPT series, and of the embedding models used for semantic search in retrieval systems.
